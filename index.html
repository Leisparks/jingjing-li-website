My Favourite City: London

London is one of my favourite cities in the world. I like London because it is a city with a long history, beautiful parks, interesting museums, wonderful music and many different cultures. During my time in London, I enjoyed walking through different neighbourhoods and discovering small streets, shops and gardens. I also loved taking the bus because I could see the city while travelling. London has many famous places, but I also like its quieter corners. There are always new things to discover, and every part of the city has its own character.

Things I Like About London

  • Beautiful parks and gardens
  • Interesting museums and galleries
  • Music, theatre and musicals
  • Historic streets and buildings
  • People from many different cultures
